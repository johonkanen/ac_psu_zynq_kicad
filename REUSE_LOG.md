**Bank 0 stays at 1.8 V, as on juliancoy** (user, 2026-10-27): VCCO_0, RSVDVCC1–3, CFGBVS (to GND) and the
PROGRAM_B/INIT_B pull-ups R9/R10 keep the source's nets and copper. So no entry is needed for them.

**SoC decaps in the block** (sheet 5, source refs), from juliancoy's power.sch: C4, C5, C8, C9, C11, C14, C15, C17–C20,
C23, C24, C26, C28–C31, C33, C34 and FB1. The rule is a footprint centre within 4 mm of the U20 body. The 3V3 caps
under U20 that juliancoy drew on other sheets are in the block too: C46, C47, C50, C55, C57, C59, C61, C65, C67, C114,
C116–C119, C121, C122. Not in the block: the bulk caps among juliancoy's regulators (C6, C12, C22, C25, C27, C32,
15–22 mm from the centre).
